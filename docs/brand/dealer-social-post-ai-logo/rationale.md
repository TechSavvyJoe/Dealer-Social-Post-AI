# Concept rationale

## Key Moment Post — selected

The white figure is the salesperson, the light-blue figure is the customer, and
the vehicle is centered between them. Their arms meet at a cyan key, making the
dealership handoff explicit. The rounded card and interaction strip make it a
social post rather than a generic automotive scene. One cyan polish point
represents AI finishing the content.

This is the first territory that communicates the application's signature
workflow without depending on the wordmark. A dedicated micro version reduces
the scene to two people, a vehicle, and the content frame below 32 px.

## Capture to Post

A phone capture transforming into a finished post explained the product in
sequence, but it required two separate scenes, pixels, and device chrome. It is
better suited to onboarding motion than a primary mark.

## Showroom Post and Dealer Spotlight

The showroom and spotlight studies clearly related to cars and dealers, but
they did not identify the salesperson-customer social workflow. They were
rejected after user review.

## Rejected abstract exploration

Publish Handoff, Editorial Frame, and Moment to Momentum were clean but generic.
They represented workflow software, not Dealer Social Post AI.

## Why the prior mark was retired

The earlier camera aperture, highway, and arrow combined three familiar visual
cliches. At favicon size the aperture collapsed while the road became a
dominant letter A.

## Preliminary naming risk

`SOCIALDEALER` is active in adjacent services and multiple adjacent products use
`DealerPost` or `DealerPost AI`. Continue design work under the working name,
but complete professional knockout, common-law, domain, and app-store clearance
before launch.
