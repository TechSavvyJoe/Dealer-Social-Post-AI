# Concept rationale — v3

## Campaign Aperture DSP — selected

The selected architecture is a responsive combination mark: a custom `DSP`
monogram for compact recognition plus the exact `Dealer Social Post AI`
wordmark for immediate trust and disambiguation.

The upright D and P use controlled, aperture-like counters and shared vertical
rhythm. They act as the stable campaign containers. The coral S moves between
them as the social/content transformation, giving the mark energy without
becoming an arrow. The construction communicates capture, shaping, and post
readiness through relationship and rhythm rather than literal illustration.

The dark editorial field and warm ivory/coral geometry are deliberately
restrained. The mark can flatten to one color, while the app-icon rendering may
use subtle gradients and depth. This separation keeps the core identity useful
for favicons, desktop icons, monochrome reproduction, and future fabrication.

## Why this route replaced v2

The v2 **Key Moment Post** mark depicted two people, a vehicle, a key, a post
card, and an AI sparkle. It explained the customer-delivery story, but too many
literal elements had to survive inside one small icon. At small sizes it read as
an illustration rather than an ownable product identity. That people/car mark,
its proofs, and its concept studies are retired under `archive/v2/` and must not
be used as the selected identity.

## Explicitly rejected generic arrow direction

An arrow/publish-route approach was rejected because an arrow primarily means
send, next, navigation, or upload. It did not distinguish Dealer Social Post AI
from generic workflow and sharing software. The v3 S supplies motion through
curve and contrast, not an arrowhead or directional glyph.

## Other prohibited fallbacks

The earlier aperture/highway combination, literal vehicle silhouettes, camera
shutters, speed lines, chat bubbles, play controls, and social-network nodes all
belong to crowded category territory. They are not alternate v3 treatments.

## Trade-offs

- `DSP` is immediately compact, but the abbreviation can mean “demand-side
  platform.” Use the full wordmark wherever space permits.
- Three overlapping dimensional letters fail at 16 px. The responsive micro
  variant intentionally switches to isolated, flat DSP glyphs at that size;
  this is a small-use substitution, not a second primary identity.
- The wordmark remains editable live text in Inter 800. That is auditable and
  correct for candidate review, but an approved outlined master is still
  required before production-ready status.

## Originality and legal boundary

The final vectors are deterministic project geometry, not a trace of a
competitor or an imitation of a named designer. This rationale is a design
decision record, not a trademark opinion. Professional clearance remains open.
