# Clear space and minimum size

Use the width of the AI polish point as the clear-space unit `x`.

- Symbol: keep at least `x` clear on every side.
- Horizontal lockup: keep `0.5x` above/below and `x` left/right.
- Do not place a dealer logo inside the product lockup's clear space.

Minimum digital sizes:

- Micro symbol: 16 px.
- Standard symbol: 32 px.
- Horizontal wordmark: 188 px wide on narrow mobile layouts.
- Stacked lockup: 160 px wide.

For sizes below 32 px, use `master/micro.svg`. Do not shrink the rounded app
tile border or editable wordmark into a favicon.
