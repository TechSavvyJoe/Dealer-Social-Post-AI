# ✅ Terminology Corrected

## Context: In-Person Dealership Conversations

This is **NOT** a phone call system. This is an AI sales coach that records **live, in-person conversations** between salespeople and customers at the dealership.

## How It Works (Corrected Understanding)

### Scenario

1. **Customer walks into dealership** 🚗
2. **Salesperson greets customer** 👋
3. **Salesperson starts recording** 🎤
   - Opens app on their device (phone/tablet)
   - Enters customer name
   - Clicks record button
4. **Live conversation happens** 💬
   - Customer asks questions about cars
   - Salesperson demonstrates features, discusses pricing
   - AI listens in real-time via microphone
5. **AI provides coaching** 🤖
   - Detects sales step (Greeting → Needs Discovery → Presentation → etc.)
   - Monitors customer sentiment
   - Sends coaching alerts ("Customer mentioned budget - address financing options")
   - Calculates success probability
6. **Conversation ends** ✋
   - Salesperson clicks "Stop" button
   - Full transcript, analysis, and performance report saved to PocketBase
7. **Manager reviews later** 👔
   - Can see all conversations from all salespeople
   - Reviews transcripts for coaching opportunities
   - Tracks team performance

### After Conversation

- ✅ **Performance report** (AI-generated coaching feedback)
- ✅ **Strengths identified**
- ✅ **Areas for improvement**
